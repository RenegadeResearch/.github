# Renegade Cybersecurity Research LLC

> **Advancing cybersecurity through independent research, secure engineering, and practical innovation.**

Welcome to **Renegade Cybersecurity Research LLC**, an independent cybersecurity research and consulting organization dedicated to advancing the field of cybersecurity through rigorous research, innovative engineering, and practical solutions.

Founded by **David Andrade**, Renegade Cybersecurity Research LLC serves as a platform for conducting original cybersecurity research, developing security-focused tools and methodologies, and providing specialized consulting on complex security challenges. Our work spans cloud computing, secure software engineering, artificial intelligence, quantum security, and enterprise cybersecurity, bridging academic research with real-world application.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Our mission is to advance cybersecurity through independent research, open collaboration, and the development of practical technologies that strengthen the security and resilience of modern computing systems.

We believe that meaningful innovation comes from combining scientific inquiry with hands-on engineering, producing work that is technically rigorous, reproducible, and valuable to both researchers and practitioners.

## 🧠 Research Philosophy

Renegade Cybersecurity Research follows a **research-first** philosophy built on the principles of technical excellence, intellectual curiosity, and practical impact.

Our objective is not simply to identify security problems, but to understand *why* they exist, develop innovative solutions, validate those solutions through experimentation, and openly contribute new knowledge to the cybersecurity community whenever possible.

Every project is approached with an emphasis on:

* Scientific rigor
* Secure engineering
* Responsible disclosure
* Reproducible research
* Open collaboration
* Practical applicability
